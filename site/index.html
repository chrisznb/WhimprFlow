Fair questions.

The honest version, including the parts that are still rough.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no seats, no paid tier waiting behind a feature. It is MIT licensed, which also means you can fork it and do your own thing with it.

macOS says the download is damaged. Is something wrong?

Nothing is wrong, and the app is not damaged. Apple charges developers a yearly fee to sign apps, and until that is in place macOS distrusts downloads from outside the App Store. Drag the app to your Applications folder, then run xattr -cr /Applications/WhimprFlow.app once in Terminal. This step disappears in an upcoming release.

Does it work offline?

Completely, once the speech model is downloaded. That download happens inside the app on first launch, one click, no terminal.

How good is it in German?

That is the part built with the most care. Filler words, mid-sentence corrections and spoken punctuation are handled specifically for German, not translated in from English. The app interface is available in German too.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are untested. Windows and Linux are not supported today.

Who is behind this?

One person, building in public. WhimprFlow started as a heavily extended fork of an open-source proof of concept by Blueturboguy07, whose MIT license and credit stay in place. It is not affiliated with Wispr Flow or any other product.
